Share your live location to any HTTP endpoint and by text message, and stop
automatically when you reach a destination you picked on a map.

Works without Google Play Services: location comes from the AOSP location
manager, maps from MapLibre, retries from WorkManager. It installs on any
device, including ones with no Google apps at all.

You decide where the data goes. Write the URL yourself, with the parameter
names and order your server expects, choosing GET, POST form or POST JSON.
Nothing is sent anywhere else: there is no account, no analytics and no
tracking of any kind.

Features:

* Sharing to an HTTP endpoint, by SMS, or both, each on its own schedule
* Destinations picked on a map, with an arrival radius; sharing stops on arrival
* Offline queue: positions are kept and sent in order when the network returns
* Adaptive cadence, tighter near the destination and looser when parked
* Rich data: accuracy, altitude, speed, heading, satellites, battery, pressure
* Persistent notification with elapsed time, distance left and last result
* Quick Settings tile, diagnostics screen, light and dark themes
* English and Italian

An optional PHP endpoint and map viewer are included in the source repository.
